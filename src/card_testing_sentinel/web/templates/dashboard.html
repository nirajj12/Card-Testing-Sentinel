Why this approach works

Sees sequences

Card testing becomes suspicious across repeated behaviour over time, rarely from one transaction on its own.

Protects genuine retries

A plain attempt counter also flags real customers retrying a declined card. Behavioural context separates them.

Decides before outcome leakage

The bank's answer for this request does not exist yet when the decision is made, so it cannot leak into it.

How it works

  1. 1Payment requestArrives before the bank is contacted.
  2. 2Behavioural historyPrior events for this device, card and IP.
  3. 344 causal featuresBuilt only from events that already happened.
  4. 4ML risk scoreCalibrated score for this request.
  5. 5Deterministic policyFixed rules turn the score into an action.
  6. 6Allow · Review · BlockReturned before authorization is sent.

⤶ The bank outcome arrives later and is never used for the current decision — it can only change what happens on the next request.

Counter vs Sentinel Why not just count attempts?

A lower counter threshold catches more attackers but disrupts far more legitimate customers. Waiting longer protects them and misses most of the attack.
