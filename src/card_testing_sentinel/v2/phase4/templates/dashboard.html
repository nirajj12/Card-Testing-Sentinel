Read the limitations with the results

  • Results are based on synthetic data.
  • The system detected no attackers within the first three attempts.
  • Median first review occurred at attempt 5.
  • Median first block occurred at attempt 7.
  • Twenty-nine of 300 blind attackers were never detected.
  • Patient and evasive attackers remain harder than burst attackers.
  • Risk score is not a guaranteed fraud probability.
  • The 760 potentially preventable attempts are an offline replay upper bound, not observed fraud prevention.
  • The local SQLite and single-process implementation is a prototype, not a production payment network.
  • Real deployment requires merchant data, monitoring, drift detection and human-review feedback.
